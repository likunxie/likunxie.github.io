Likun Xie

E-mail: xie@mpim-bonn.mpg.de

I am a postdoctoral fellow at the Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in Bonn. I completed my Ph.D. at the University of Illinois Urbana–Champaign in May 2025. My research interests are in number theory and commutative algebra. Recently, I have been particularly interested in geometric approaches to arithmetic problems in number theory and homological questions in commutative algebra. CV
